01 / A little context

Understanding before application.

I am an undergraduate Physics student at the University of Dodoma, Tanzania. My curiosity lies in understanding how mathematical structures describe physical reality, and how theoretical ideas meet experiment.


I am particularly interested in fundamental physics, mathematical methods, computational modelling and scientific instrumentation. Alongside academic study, I enjoy building, designing and communicating ideas across scientific and creative disciplines.
